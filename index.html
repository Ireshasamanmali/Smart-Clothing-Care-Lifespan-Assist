Weather-Adaptive Care

Sri Lanka's tropical monsoons and high ambient humidity require specialized laundry habits to avoid mold and odor.

Monsoon Season Protocol

During heavy monsoonal rain, clothes take twice as long to dry. Utilize an extra high-speed spin cycle in your washing machine to extract 80% of moisture before hanging. Avoid drying damp items closely together.

💡 Pro Tip: Add 2 tablespoons of white vinegar to the final rinse to neutralize musty mildew spores before they develop.

ISO 3758 Symbol & Fabric Guide

Master standard international care labels to avoid permanent fabric damage.

🧺 Washing Symbols

The wash tub icon indicates max water temperature. A bar under the tub signals gentle synthetic or wool mechanical action.

☀️ Drying Symbols

A square with a circle represents tumble drying. Diagonal lines in the top corner mean line dry in the shade only.

🏷️ Ironing Symbols

Dots inside the iron indicate heat limit: 1 dot (110°C Synthetics), 2 dots (150°C Wool/Silk), 3 dots (200°C Cotton/Linen).
